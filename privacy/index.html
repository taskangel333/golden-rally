Golden Point privacy policy

Effective: 27 October 2026

Golden Point keeps score of padel matches on Apple Watch and shows your past matches on iPhone. On the App Store it is called Golden Rally. It is made by Nishanth Suresh, an individual developer in the Netherlands. This policy explains what the app reads, what it stores, where that information goes, and what happens if you contact me.

The short version

  • Golden Point has no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your matches stay on your Apple Watch and your iPhone. They move only between those two devices and are not included in iCloud or computer backups.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. Golden Point never sees your payment details.

What Golden Point reads from Apple Health

When you start a match, Golden Point asks for permission to use Apple Health. If you allow it, the app:

  • reads your heart rate and your active and resting energy while a match is running, to show them on the watch and to add them up at the end of the match
  • saves each match you keep as a workout in Apple Health, so it counts towards your activity like any other workout

Golden Point does not read any other Health data. Golden Point still keeps score without this permission. The heart rate and energy fields then stay empty.

What Golden Point stores

  • On your Apple Watch: the match in progress (so it survives a restart), the number of free matches used, the last match format you chose, and whether you have bought Golden Point Pro.
  • On your iPhone: the matches you saved on the watch (date and time, format, score, sets, and the duration, heart rate and energy of the workout), and whether you have bought Golden Point Pro.

When you save a match on the watch, it is sent to your iPhone over Apple's WatchConnectivity link between your paired watch and iPhone. It is not sent anywhere else. Golden Point does not use location and does not store where you played.

Match history is not included in device backups, so a new or restored iPhone starts with an empty match history. The workouts themselves stay in Apple Health. A match in progress on the watch is not backed up either. Small settings, such as the number of free matches used, the last match format and whether you bought Pro, can be part of your own device backups.

Golden Point keeps your match history until you delete the app.

What Golden Point does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No server of its own. The app does not send your data over the internet.
  • No analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing is shared with third parties.

Golden Point Pro and purchases

Golden Point Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment and tells the app whether you have bought Pro. Golden Point never sees your payment details or your Apple Account details.

Crash reports and statistics from Apple

Golden Point contains no analytics or crash reporting code. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass crash reports and statistics about Golden Point on to me. Apple says it shares these only in aggregated form or in a form that does not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share With App Developers.

If you email me

If you write to the support address, I receive your email address, your name if you include it, and what you write. I use this only to answer you and to fix problems you report. I never use it for marketing. My legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering questions about Golden Point (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete support emails within a year after our conversation ends, unless I need one longer for a legal claim.

The support address is a Gmail account, so Google stores these emails for me, possibly on servers in the United States.

You can object to this use of your emails at any time. Write to me and I will delete them, unless I need one for a legal claim.

This website

This page and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, under GitHub's own privacy statement. I do not receive these logs. The pages use no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Health data and your consent

Everything Golden Point reads from Apple Health, and everything it stores, stays on your devices and in your own backups of them. I cannot see it and never receive it. If the law treats me as responsible for this processing anyway, it rests on your explicit consent, which you give in Apple's permission screens (Article 9(2)(a) and Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time as described below. Giving access is your choice. Golden Point keeps score without it.

Your choices

Health access: you can turn Golden Point's access to each Health category on or off at any time in the Health app on your iPhone. Tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Golden Point.

Delete your data: deleting the app from a device deletes what Golden Point stored on that device. To remove it everywhere, delete Golden Point from both your Apple Watch and your iPhone. Older backups keep a copy of the small settings until they are replaced or deleted. Workouts already saved in Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Your rights

You can ask me for access to, correction of or deletion of personal data I hold about you, ask me to restrict or stop using it, and ask for a copy in a portable form. For data on your devices, you already hold it: you can see it in Golden Point and delete it by deleting the app. For support emails, write to the address below. I answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), or to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. If a child emails me, I use the email only to reply and delete it as described above.
